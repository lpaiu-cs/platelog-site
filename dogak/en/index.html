Dogak Dogak

A keyboard you can feel

How to use & support · Privacy

An iPhone keyboard with key sounds and haptics in five materials, and a cute buddy sitting on top.

Linear switches, clicky switches, wood, silicone and wax: each material has its own sound, haptics and keycap motion. Put one buddy on your keyboard: Cheese the cat, Gomgom the bear, Somi the bunny, Ppiyak the chick, Bori the puppy or Mochi the rice cake.

This version is free. There are no ads, trials, subscriptions or in-app purchases.

What you type is never stored or sent off your iPhone. The app and keyboard have no network features.

How to use & supportPrivacy

Requires an iPhone with iOS 16 or later. The keyboard types Korean (2-set) and English. The app's screens are in Korean.
