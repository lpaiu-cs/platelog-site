Dogak Dogak privacy

Effective November 14, 2026 · About · How to use & support

Operator and contact

Dogak Dogak (the keyboard is named Dogak) is provided by JUNEYOUNG KIM, an independent developer based in South Korea, using the developer name lpaiu. Privacy contact and complaints: lpaiu.cs@gmail.com.

What we do not collect

The app and keyboard do not collect personal information. Text you type with the keyboard goes only to the app you are typing in; Dogak does not store it or send it off your device. The app and keyboard have no network features, account, advertising, analytics or tracking. In password fields iOS switches to the standard keyboard, so Dogak never receives passwords.

Allow Full Access

iOS requires this permission for a keyboard to play sounds and haptics and to share the settings you choose in the app with the keyboard. Dogak uses it only for those two purposes. The warning shown when you turn it on is the same text iOS shows for every keyboard. You can type without it.

What stays on your device

Dogak saves settings such as your material and buddy, sound and haptic preferences and the Korean/English state, plus a small record that lets the keyboard restart safely after a crash (recent crash count and time, app version). It does not save what you type. This information never leaves your device and is removed when you delete the app.

Children

Because Dogak collects no personal information, it receives no information from any user, including children.

Email support and this website

If you email support, the developer receives your email address and what you choose to send. It is used only to answer and troubleshoot, and is kept only as long as needed for that purpose and any legal obligation. Email is processed by Google's email service and handled in South Korea. Please do not send passwords or the contents of what you type. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security. See the website and support privacy notice.

Your rights

To access, correct, delete or restrict information the developer holds, such as support emails, email the address above. We respond within applicable legal deadlines, free of charge. Settings stored on your device are not accessible to the developer and are deleted with the app. Where applicable law provides it, you may also complain to your local privacy authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date and announce it on this page and in the app's update notes. This is a translation; if it differs from the Korean version, the Korean version prevails.
