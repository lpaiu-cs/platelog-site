Dodak Dodak

How to use & support

About · Privacy

Turn on the keyboard

  1. Go to Settings › General › Keyboard › Keyboards › Add New Keyboard and choose Dodak (shown as 도닥 or ドダク on a Korean or Japanese iPhone).
  2. Tap Dodak in the list and turn on Allow Full Access. It is needed for sounds and haptics; you can type without it.
  3. While typing, touch and hold the globe key below the keyboard and choose Dodak.

In the Dodak app you can try the keyboard right away, without turning it on.

Using it

  • Switch materials with the small keycaps along the top. Tap the buddy to change to the next one.
  • Hold the delete key to delete repeatedly.
  • Drag the space bar sideways to move the cursor. In English and Korean, double-tap space to type a period.
  • Email and address fields start in English; number fields start on the number pad.
  • Change volume, haptic strength and the buddy's sound on Return in the app's settings.

Choose keyboard languages

  • Tap the globe button on the app's first screen to add, remove and reorder keyboards (English, Korean, Japanese).
  • With more than one, the language key (for example 한/A or あ/A) moves through them in that order. With only one, the language key goes away.
  • At first you get Korean and English in Korea, Japanese and English in Japan, and English elsewhere, based on your iPhone's language and region.
  • Without Allow Full Access the keyboard can't read this setting and keeps its default keyboards.

Japanese input

  • Flick input is the default. To type in romaji, turn off “Flick input for Japanese” on the globe button's screen.
  • Conversion candidates appear above the keys as you type. Tap one, or press space to move to the next candidate and Return to confirm.
  • Because Dodak does not learn from what you type, the order of candidates does not change with use.

No sound

  • Make sure Allow Full Access is on.
  • Sounds are muted in Silent mode. To hear them anyway, turn on “Play in Silent mode” in the app's settings.
  • Volume follows your media volume. You can also mute with the speaker button at the top of the keyboard.

No haptics

Check Allow Full Access and the haptic strength in the app's settings. Haptics do not play when System Haptics is off in iOS Settings › Sounds & Haptics.

The keyboard switches to another keyboard

iOS temporarily uses the standard keyboard in password and phone number fields; Dodak returns in other fields. After reinstalling the app, iOS may switch back to the default keyboard: choose Dodak again with the globe key.

“Sounds and effects are off for a while” appears

The keyboard stopped right after opening more than once, so it is running without sounds and effects for a few hours to stay stable. It returns to normal automatically. If it keeps happening, please let us know.

A gray background above the keyboard

From iOS 26, the system draws a background around keyboards. Dodak is designed so its material and buddy row sits naturally on it.

Reduce Motion

Turn on Reduce Motion in Settings › Accessibility › Motion to calm the key effects.

Requirements

iPhone with iOS 16 or later. iPad is not supported. Types English, Korean (2-set) and Japanese (flick or romaji). The app is available in English, Korean and Japanese.
