How to use & support

About · Privacy

Turn on the keyboard

  1. Go to Settings › General › Keyboard › Keyboards › Add New Keyboard and choose 도각 (Dogak).
  2. Tap 도각 in the list and turn on Allow Full Access. It is needed for sounds and haptics; you can type without it.
  3. While typing, touch and hold the globe key below the keyboard and choose 도각.

In the Dogak app you can try the keyboard right away, without turning it on.

Using it

  • Switch materials with the small keycaps along the top. Tap the buddy to change to the next one.
  • Hold the delete key to delete repeatedly.
  • Drag the space bar sideways to move the cursor. Double-tap space to type a period.
  • The 한/영 key switches between Korean and English. Email and address fields start in English; number fields start on the number pad.
  • Change volume, haptic strength and the buddy's sound on Return in the app's settings.

No sound

  • Make sure Allow Full Access is on.
  • Sounds are muted in Silent mode. To hear them anyway, turn on “무음 모드에서도 소리 내기” (Play in Silent mode) in the app's settings.
  • Volume follows your media volume. You can also mute with the speaker button at the top of the keyboard.

No haptics

Check Allow Full Access and the haptic strength in the app's settings. Haptics do not play when System Haptics is off in iOS Settings › Sounds & Haptics.

The keyboard switches to another keyboard

iOS temporarily uses the standard keyboard in password and phone number fields; Dogak returns in other fields. After reinstalling the app, iOS may switch back to the default keyboard: choose 도각 again with the globe key.

“잠시 소리와 효과를 꺼 두었어요” appears

The keyboard stopped right after opening more than once, so it is running without sounds and effects for a few hours to stay stable. It returns to normal automatically. If it keeps happening, please let us know.

A gray background above the keyboard

From iOS 26, the system draws a background around keyboards. Dogak is designed so its material and buddy row sits naturally on it.

Reduce Motion

Turn on Reduce Motion in Settings › Accessibility › Motion to calm the key effects.

Requirements

iPhone with iOS 16 or later. iPad is not supported. Types Korean (2-set) and English. The app's screens are in Korean.
