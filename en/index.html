One photo before you eat. That's all.

An iPhone app for sharing meal photos with a small crew of 2 to 6 friends, partners, or family

Privacy PolicyTerms of UseSupport

Snap a photo before you eat and post it, and your crew sees it and leaves a comment. Open the camera, press the shutter, and the photo goes onto a page of your crew's shared diary. Only members of the same crew see the photos.
