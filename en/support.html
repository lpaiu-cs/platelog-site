Frequently asked questions

Joining a crewThe leader creates an invite code in Settings → Crew tab and sends it to you. After you sign in, enter it in "Join with invite code". A code works for 7 days.
Who sees my photos?Only members of the same crew. If you posted a photo to several crews, only those crews.
Deleting a photoOpen the photo and tap "Delete" at the top right. If you posted it to several crews, choose whether to remove it from one crew only or from all of them.
Too many notificationsTurn them off by type in Settings → Me → Notifications. If you set quiet hours, notifications during those hours arrive together in the morning.
Deleting your accountSettings → Me → Delete account. Your photos and messages are deleted too, and this can't be undone. If you can't use the app, see the account deletion guide.
