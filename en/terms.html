Terms of Use

Effective October 10, 2026 · platelog · Privacy Policy · Support

1. The service

platelog is an app where friends, family, and acquaintances share meal photos and messages in a small crew (up to 6 people). It's free and has no ads. You can join a crew only with an invite code, and photos, messages, and reactions are seen only by members of the same crew.

2. Your account

You sign in with an Apple or Google account. You can link more than one sign-in method to one account. You're responsible for your account and devices.

3. What you must not post

Don't post or send any of the following. Such content and users are not tolerated.

  • Sexual photos or nudity
  • Violent or gory photos, or content that encourages self-harm
  • Hate, discrimination, harassment, abuse, or threats aimed at a person or group
  • Photos taken without someone's consent, or other people's private lives or personal information
  • Illegal activity, or selling, buying, or promoting illegal goods
  • Ads or spam, or impersonating someone else

4. Reporting and blocking

  • Report: Settings → Crew tab → "…" next to the member's name → Report (Inappropriate photo · Abuse or harassment · Something else). The other person isn't told that you reported them.
  • Block: tap Block in the same menu. When you block someone, their photos, messages, and reactions, and notifications caused by them, disappear for you right away. They aren't told, and you can unblock them anytime.
  • Leader: The leader can remove members from the crew. Anyone can delete their own photos and messages and leave the crew.

5. What you post

The rights to photos and messages belong to the person who posted them. platelog stores and delivers them only as far as needed to show them to crew members. When you delete them, they disappear for members, and the files are deleted shortly after (Privacy Policy section 5).

6. Deleting your account

You can delete it right away in the app under Settings → Me → Delete account. If you can't use the app, request it by email: Account deletion guide

7. Changes to and end of the service

Because it's a free service, features may change or the service may end. If it ends, we'll let you know in the app in advance and let you export your photos before then (Settings → Me → Export my photos).
