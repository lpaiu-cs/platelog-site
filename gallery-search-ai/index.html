Gallery Search AI

Describe it. Find it.

Type what you remember about a photo. AI brings matching photos to the top, ready to share or copy.

Gallery Search AI searches the photos you choose, in English, on your iPhone. The AI models are included in the app. There is no developer search server, separate app account, advertising or analytics service.

Confirm the free App Store transaction to try AI search and photo preparation for seven days. Afterwards, unlock those features with one purchase: US$1.99 in the US storefront. Your local price is shown before purchase. There is no subscription or automatic charge.
