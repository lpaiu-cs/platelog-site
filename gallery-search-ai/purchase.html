Seven-day free trial

Tap Start 7-day free trial and confirm the free App Store transaction. The seven days begin at the original confirmed transaction time, not when the app is first opened. The free trial is a non-consumable purchase named 7-day Trial. Reinstalling, clearing the photo collection or restoring purchases does not restart it. No subscription begins and no automatic charge is made when it ends.

What changes after the trial

AI search, preparing newly added photos and rebuilding the search index require the permanent unlock. Browsing already saved photos, opening previews, Share, Copy, removing app data and reading support/privacy information remain available.

Lifetime Access

Lifetime Access is a one-time non-consumable App Store purchase with a US storefront price of US$1.99. Apple shows your local price and any applicable taxes before you confirm. It unlocks this app's AI search and photo preparation on compatible iPhones without recurring charges. It is not a promise of every future feature, compatibility with every future device or operating system, or perfect search results. Mandatory rights to updates or other remedies that apply to you are preserved.

Restore, refunds and problems
